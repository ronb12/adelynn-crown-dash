Privacy Policy — Crown Dash: Princess Run

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Crown Dash does not operate its own accounts or run third‑party ads or analytics in this web and app build.

Game Center

If you use the iOS app and sign in to Game Center (Apple), features such as leaderboards and achievements are provided through Apple’s Game Center service. That activity is subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy and how Game Center handles player identifiers and gameplay results. The developer does not receive your Apple ID email from Game Center for routine leaderboard use.

Data this build does not use directly

  • No in‑app account login beyond Game Center on iOS (optional, Apple‑managed).
  • No location tracking for advertising.
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or payment card numbers inside the shipped game experience described in this repository.
  • No third‑party advertising or analytics SDKs in this repository.

If advertising, additional analytics, in‑app purchases, or cloud saves beyond Game Center are added later, this policy should be updated before wider distribution.
