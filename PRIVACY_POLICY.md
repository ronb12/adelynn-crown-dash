# Privacy Policy — Crown Dash: Princess Run

Crown Dash does not operate its own accounts or run third‑party ads or analytics in this build.

## Game Center

If you sign in to **Game Center** (Apple), features such as leaderboards and achievements are provided through **Apple’s Game Center service**. That activity is subject to [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and how Game Center handles player identifiers and gameplay results. The developer does not receive your Apple ID email from Game Center for routine leaderboard use.

## Data this build does not use directly

- No in‑app account login beyond Game Center (optional, Apple‑managed).
- No location tracking for advertising.
- No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or payment card numbers inside the game binary as shipped here.
- No third‑party advertising or analytics SDKs in this repository.

If advertising, additional analytics, in‑app purchases, or cloud saves beyond Game Center are added later, this policy should be updated before App Store submission.
