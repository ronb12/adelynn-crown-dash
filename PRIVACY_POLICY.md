# Privacy Policy — Crown Dash: Princess Run

Crown Dash does not collect personal information in this prototype build.

This build:
- Does not require account login.
- Does not track location.
- Does not collect contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or payment data.
- Does not use third-party ads or analytics yet.

If ads, analytics, in-app purchases, leaderboards, or cloud saves are added later, this privacy policy must be updated before App Store submission.
